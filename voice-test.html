How to test
  1. Read the first question, then pause for five seconds. Scrolling should hold.
  2. Read the second sentence slowly, then speed up. Your current words should stay visible.
  3. Say “I am taking a quick break from the script.” The position should hold until you return to the script.
  4. Finish the paragraph, including “hashing”, “salts”, and “peppers”. Check the recognised words below the script.
  5. If it loses your place, stop, tap the word you want to read next, and tap Follow my voice again.
  6. Repeat in your usual teleprompter position, then try a longer script for five minutes.

Use Safari on the iPad over HTTPS. If no speech arrives, check microphone permission and that Siri is enabled. Start in Safari before testing a Home Screen shortcut.
